## Optional exact-solver evaluation

`requirements-oracle.txt` pins
[`connect-four-ai==1.0.0`](https://github.com/benjaminrall/connect-four-ai),
an MIT-licensed external solver by Benjamin Rall. It is used only to score the
fixed evaluation positions generated by `scripts/benchmark_solver_moves.py`; it is
not part of training or Space inference, and no solver source or binary is stored
in this repository.

The solver benchmark's checked-in JSON/Markdown outputs are evaluation results
produced through that API, not redistributed solver code.
